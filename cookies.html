Cookie & storage policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Short version

This website’s own code does not set any cookies, and it has no advertising, analytics or tracking. Because of that, there is no cookie banner: there is nothing that needs your consent.

What is stored on your device

The site uses your browser’s local storage (not cookies) for two small items:

  • shortly-key: a random secret key created in your browser. It is needed so you can edit or delete the links you create. It stays until you clear this site’s data. This is strictly necessary for that feature, which you ask for by creating a link.
  • shortly-base: only saved if you change the “short-link base” field, so the site can remember your choice.

Neither item is sent to advertisers or analytics services. The key is sent to our service only to load your own links or to create, edit or delete a link, and the service stores only a hash of it (see the privacy policy).

Third parties

The site does not load third-party scripts, fonts, embeds or trackers. Our hosting providers (GitHub and Cloudflare) may use technical cookies or logs on their own systems for security and delivery, which we do not control; see their privacy policies linked in our privacy policy.

Your choices

You can delete the stored items at any time in your browser’s settings (clear site data). If you delete the key without saving a copy, you will lose the ability to edit or delete links you made with it.

If this changes

If we ever add analytics, advertising or any other non-essential storage, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.
